About this archive

Namjoon's Booklist is an independent project built around RM's extraordinary reading life. Since his debut, Kim Namjoon has quietly inspired millions of people to read — through a bookshelf Instagram post, a late-night VLIVE, a page shared on a story.

Every entry here carries a source: the interview, broadcast, or post where the book surfaced. If we can't trace it, we don't list it.

This archive stands alongside the great work of Namjoon's Library and other fan projects — we encourage you to explore them all.
