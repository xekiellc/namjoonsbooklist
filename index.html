About this archive

Namjoon's Booklist is an independent, non-commercial fan project. RM's reading life has inspired millions of people to pick up books — this site exists to make that reading list easy to browse, search, and verify.

Every entry in the catalog carries a source: the interview, broadcast, Instagram post, or appearance where the book was mentioned or spotted. If we can't trace it, we don't list it.

This archive stands alongside the great work of other fan projects — including the long-running Namjoon's Library — and we encourage you to explore them all.

Artwork on this site is illustrated fan art, not official photography. Book titles and cover art belong to their respective publishers and authors.
